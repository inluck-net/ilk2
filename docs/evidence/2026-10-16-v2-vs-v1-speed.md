# V2 against V1: speed on the projects with a V1 baseline (Oct 16)

Method (docs/04 §4.5): `ilk2 report --project P --v1-baseline` on each host. V1's numbers come from its own close records for the same repository. V2's are counted per **work** step: steps of verification plans are excluded, because ilk2 accepts them from their gates in about a minute, and counting them would flatter V2 (kira-live's headline `minutes_per_accepted_step` is 13.3 with them in).

| Project | V1 (Oct 12, one day) | V2 (Oct 13-16) |
|---|---|---|
| kira resolver (rezmac `kira-live`) | 37.3 min per step advanced; 30 iterations, 11 steps; 19 of 30 iterations advanced nothing | 30.1 min per work step; 11 shipped batches, 17 work steps; 10% of attempts produced nothing; submit-to-ship median 34 min (mean 55) |
| gh-resolve development (chad-mbp) | 74.4 min per step advanced; 14 iterations, 9 steps; 5 of 14 advanced nothing | 38.9 min per work step; 14 batches, 46 work steps; 8% of attempts produced nothing |

Not comparable yet:
- ilk-skills: 0 V2 batches since the takeover. Its V1 baseline is 65.9 min per step.
- gh-triage and kira-shadow: no V1 baseline.

Caveats:
- V1's sample is one day, on different issues, and its steps differ in size.
- V2's gh-resolve figure includes P5 and P6, which ran while ilk2 defects were fixed mid-batch: 57 human transitions, 40 in one batch.
- kira-live's 2 human waits had a median of 368 min, because #8519's first batch hit its gate cap twice and waited 735 min overnight. The RSI `gate-cap` detector now finds that pattern.
